## The story

Greenstead is the operator group behind a small family of Bangkok hospitality, retail, and lifestyle brands. Rather than chase scale for its own sake, the team builds places they'd want to work in themselves — and runs them with the same standards across the board.

## The culture

Because the people behind Greenstead are hands-on operators, the businesses share a practical, no-nonsense culture: clear expectations, fair pay handled properly, and real investment in training. Staff who do well move between concepts and grow with the group.

## What they're known for

Consistency. Walk into any of their venues and the standard of service, the care for the team, and the attention to detail feel the same — because the operators are the same.
