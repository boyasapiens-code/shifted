## The story

Stash BKK was built by operators who wanted a cannabis retail experience that felt calm, knowledgeable, and genuinely welcoming — closer to a good specialty café than a dispensary. The bet was simple: treat customers like guests and staff like professionals, and the rest follows.

## The culture

Budtenders here are trained, not just rostered. New team members learn the product properly, work alongside experienced staff, and are trusted to run their section. Schedules are published ahead, the service-charge split is clear, and good work gets noticed out loud. It's a place people stay — and come back to.

## What they're known for

Knowledgeable, unrushed service and a room that doesn't feel like a transaction. Regulars come for the people as much as the product.
