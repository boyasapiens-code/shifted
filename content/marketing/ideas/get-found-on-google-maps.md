When someone within walking distance searches for what you sell, you either show up or you don't. Most small venues lose those customers without ever knowing. Here's where to start — no budget required.

## 1. Claim and complete the profile

Your Google Business Profile is your real homepage. Fill in every field: hours, category, services, a real phone, the menu. Half-finished profiles rank below complete ones, full stop.

## 2. Make reviews a habit, not a hope

A steady trickle of recent, genuine reviews beats a big pile of old ones. Build one simple ask into the close of a good visit. Reply to every review — the good and the bad — calmly.

## 3. Fresh photos, regularly

Google favours active listings. New photos every couple of weeks — the room, the food, the team — signal you're open and cared for.

## 4. Post like it's a free billboard

The "Posts" feature is underused. A weekly update about a special, an event, or new hours keeps your listing alive in the ranking.

## 5. Be consistent everywhere

Your name, address and phone should match exactly across Google, your site, and your socials. Mismatches quietly tank your local ranking.
