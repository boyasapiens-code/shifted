# Know Your Rights

Six categories cover the full life of a job. Hospitality & retail get their own category
because that's Shifted's core audience and where the gray areas bite hardest.

## Categories

| id | name | scope |
|----|------|-------|
| `pay-and-money` | Pay & money | Minimum wage, overtime, service charge & tips, lawful deductions, payslips, social security, bonuses. |
| `time-and-rest` | Time & rest | Working hours, breaks, weekly day off, public holidays, annual leave, sick leave, personal leave, maternity & family leave. |
| `starting-a-job` | Starting a job | Employment contracts, probation, what must be agreed in writing, work-from-home rules, foreign worker basics. |
| `ending-a-job` | Ending a job | Resignation, termination, notice periods, severance pay, final pay, what counts as fair dismissal. |
| `conduct-and-disputes` | Conduct & disputes | Warnings & discipline, workplace harassment, unfair dismissal, how the Labour Court works, where to complain. |
| `hospitality-and-retail` | Hospitality & retail | Service charge distribution, tips, shift work, daily/casual workers, split shifts, uniform & training costs. |
